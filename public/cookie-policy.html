Cookie Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Room Finder Platform • Bihar, India

Privacy-First Philosophy: Room Finder does not track you across the internet, sell your personal browsing habits, or deploy commercial advertising cookies. We only use strictly necessary cookies required to authenticate and secure your account.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your computer, tablet, or mobile phone as you browse. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your login session, and provide security against unauthorized account access.

2. Our Minimalist Cookie Approach

Unlike many online marketplaces, Room Finder follows a strictly minimal data and cookie policy:

  • Zero Advertising Trackers: We do not run third-party advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or behavioral profiling tools (such as Facebook Pixel, Google Ads remarketing, or ad network trackers).
  • Zero Invasive Analytics: We do not sell or transmit user browsing telemetry to data brokers.
  • Strictly Essential Cookies Only: We exclusively store cookies that are strictly necessary for core application functionality, specifically session authentication and security.

3. Detailed List of Cookies Used

Below is a transparent breakdown of the exact cookie utilized by the Room Finder platform:

Cookie Name Category Duration Security Flags Purpose & Description
rf_token Strictly Necessary 7 Days HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax Stores an encrypted JSON Web Token (JWT) verifying that you are logged into your tenant, owner, or admin account. The HttpOnly attribute ensures malicious browser scripts cannot read or steal your session token.

4. Local Storage Usage

In addition to the essential authentication cookie, Room Finder uses browser localStorage for client-side interface state management:

  • rf_cookie_consent: Stores your preference when you acknowledge our cookie notification banner so the banner does not repeatedly appear on every page visit.

Local storage data remains strictly on your device and is never shared with third parties.

5. Do You Need Cookie Consent for These Cookies?

Under international privacy regulations (including the EU ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC Article 5(3), the General Data Protection Regulation / GDPR, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023):

  • Consent Exemption for Essential Cookies: Prior opt-in consent is not required for cookies that are strictly necessary to deliver an online service explicitly requested by the user (such as authenticating your login session and keeping you securely signed in).
  • Our Transparency Commitment: Even though a blocking consent modal is not legally mandatory for essential authentication cookies, we display an informative notification banner across the website to ensure complete transparency with all room seekers, students, and property owners.

6. How to Manage or Disable Cookies

You have full control over cookies through your web browser settings. You can configure your browser to block, warn, or delete cookies at any time:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Settings → Safari → Privacy & Security → Block All Cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

Note: If you disable the rf_token cookie in your browser, you will not be able to log in to your owner dashboard, manage property listings, or access your account profile.

7. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or technical changes. Any revisions will be published immediately on this page with an updated effective date.
