Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Room Finder Platform

Privacy Commitment: Room Finder is committed to protecting your personal information. This privacy policy transparently outlines how data is handled across the application, ensuring trust, privacy, and security for all tenants and room owners.

1. Information We Collect

Room Finder collects only the information necessary to provide accommodation search and listing services in Bihar:

  • Visitor Browsing Data: Public visitors can browse listings, search by city or rent, and view room details without registering an account. No tracking cookies or external ad trackers are used.
  • Account Registration Details: When you register as a user, student, or property owner, we collect your full name, email address, an optional 10-digit mobile phone number, and a chosen password.
  • Property Listing Information: When an owner creates a listing, we collect property titles, room types, rent amount, security deposit, address, city, locality, geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude), amenities, descriptions, and up to four uploaded property photos.
  • Authentication Credentials: Passwords are never stored in plaintext; they are securely encrypted using industry-standard one-way cryptographic hashing before storage.

2. Email Verification & Password Reset Emails

To prevent spam and ensure account security:

  • During registration and password recovery, a 6-digit verification code (OTP) is generated securely on the server.
  • The verification code is sent directly to your registered email address.
  • Verification codes are stored securely with a strict 10-minute expiration period. Plaintext passwords and codes are never stored in our systems and are never logged to public consoles.
  • Once used or expired, verification tokens are immediately invalidated.

3. How Your Information Is Used

The information collected is used solely for the operational purposes of the Room Finder website:

  • To authenticate registered users, property owners, and administrators.
  • To publish rental accommodation listings across Bihar on our public search index.
  • To display owner contact information to prospective tenants and students seeking accommodation.
  • To securely maintain user sessions and authenticated access.

4. Visibility of Contact Information

Because Room Finder is a direct rental discovery platform without intermediary brokers:

  • The contact mobile number provided by a property owner is displayed openly on the room detail page so prospective tenants can contact the owner directly.
  • Property owners consent to this public display when publishing a listing.
  • Demonstration listings use clearly fictional and visibly masked phone numbers (e.g. +91 00000 00001 (DEMO ONLY)) to ensure no real individual is contacted.

5. Third-Party Services & Integrations

Room Finder integrates with selected external tools and open services:

  • Interactive Location Maps: We provide interactive maps to display approximate property locations for room seekers. Map attribution is provided to OpenStreetMap contributors in compliance with applicable copyright guidelines.
  • Email Notifications: Account verification and password reset emails are delivered securely through authorized email delivery infrastructure.
  • Listing Media: Demonstration accommodations utilize original vector illustrations and approved preview photography.

6. Data Storage & Security Measures

We take software security and user data integrity seriously:

  • Secure Database: All data is stored in isolated, production-grade cloud databases using authenticated, encrypted connections with strict firewall rules.
  • Password Protection: Strong cryptographic hashing protects passwords against unauthorized extraction or inspection.
  • Session Security: Authentication credentials and tokens are delivered inside secure, HTTP-only browser cookies to protect against unauthorized script access.
  • Rate Limiting: Authentication endpoints (login, registration, OTP requests) are protected by rate limiters to deter brute-force and credential-stuffing attacks.
  • File Upload Guardrails: Uploaded photographs are restricted to JPG, PNG, and WEBP formats with a 2 MB size limit per file, using cryptographically generated unique filenames.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

Room Finder uses only strictly necessary authentication cookies (rf_token) to ensure secure account access and protect against session hijacking. We do not use third-party advertising, retargeting, or tracking cookies. For complete details on our cookie usage and browser controls, please read our full Cookie Policy.

8. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Property owners can edit or permanently delete their listings at any time through their owner dashboard. Deleting a listing immediately removes the property record and unlinks associated photograph files from the server. Users wishing to delete their account or have their data removed can contact our support team.
